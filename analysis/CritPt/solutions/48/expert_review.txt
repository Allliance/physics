Question: Is the challenge well-defined and technically correct? If not, please specify the issue in "Other"
Answer:
The analytic continuation is not uniquely determined by the values of $Z(n,\eta)$ at positive integer $n$, and different analytic interpolations that agree at all integers can give different values of $\left.\partial_n Z(n,\eta)\right|_{n=1}$. Thus, the integer-replica data determine the values of $Z$ at integer $n$ but do not, by themselves, determine the derivative at $n=1$.

Question: Solution review notes
Answer:
The solution accurately accounted for the fact that analytic continuation is not unique. However, more generally, c(\eta) should be replaced by c(n,\eta). Note that this n-dependence does not affect the first derivative at n=1, since the term involving \partial_n c(n,\eta) is multiplied by \sin[\pi(n-1)]and therefore vanishes there, but mathematically it is better practice to include the n-dependence.
