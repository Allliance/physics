\subsubsection*{Problem}
\paragraph*{Problem setup}
Consider fermion fields that transform in the adjoint representation of a $U(N)$ gauge group. Suppose the fields carry a $U(1)$ charge whose fugacity is $q$. A generating function of the Witten index, for example, could be $I_N(q)=1-q+q^2+...$, which means the index at charge 1 is -1 and the index at charge 2 is 1.

\paragraph*{Main problem}
Compute the generating function of the index of trace relations up to charge 15 in a free $U(2)$ gauge theory that contains only two adjoint fields: a charge 1 fermion $\psi$, and its derivative field $\partial\psi$ with charge 2.
