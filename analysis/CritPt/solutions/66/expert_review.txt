Question: Is the challenge well-defined and technically correct? If not, please specify the issue in "Other"
Answer:
I was unhappy with the problem statement that there was a "U(1) R-charge" but that \psi and \partial\psi have different charges. Typically in high energy physics an R-charge commutes with a spacetime derivative (implied by the \partial). I feel the problem might be less confusing if it just said "U(1) charge" rather than "U(1) R-charge". I also thought it would clearer to say that the fermion fields *transform* in the adjoint representation, rather than say that the fields are the adjoint representation.
