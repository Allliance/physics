\subsubsection*{Problem}
\paragraph*{Problem setup}
Consider the Kitaev honeycomb model at the isotropic limit (assuming $J_x=J_y=J_z=1$) on a 3x2 Bravais lattice with periodic boundary conditions.

{\color{blue}
To fix the normalization and sign convention unambiguously, take
\[
H=-\sum_{\langle ij\rangle_x}\sigma_i^x\sigma_j^x
  -\sum_{\langle ij\rangle_y}\sigma_i^y\sigma_j^y
  -\sum_{\langle ij\rangle_z}\sigma_i^z\sigma_j^z,
\]
where $\sigma_i^\alpha$ denotes the standard $2\times2$ Pauli matrix acting on the local spin-$\tfrac12$ Hilbert space at site $i$.
}

\paragraph*{Main problem}
How many degenerate ground states are there? How many of them are in the flux-free sector? Compute the energy of the ground states with three decimal precision.
