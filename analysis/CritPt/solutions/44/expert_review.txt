Question: Is the challenge well-defined and technically correct? If not, please specify the issue in "Other"
Answer:
The original problem statement has a material normalization ambiguity because it does not write the Hamiltonian or distinguish Pauli matrices $\sigma^\alpha$ from spin operators $S^\alpha=\sigma^\alpha/2$. Both conventions are common. They give ground-state energies that differ by a factor of four. An automated checker expecting a unique numerical energy could therefore reject a physically valid answer. I revised `problem.tex` to state the Hamiltonian, including its sign, explicitly.
