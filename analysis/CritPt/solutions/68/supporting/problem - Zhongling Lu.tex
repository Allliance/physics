\subsubsection*{Problem}
\paragraph*{Problem setup}

Let $\rho$ and $\sigma$ be density operators on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, and assume that $\sigma>0$. The quantum $f$-divergence is defined by 
\begin{align*}
    D^{\mathrm{std}}_f(\rho \|\sigma) =  \int_0^\infty \mathrm{tr}\bigl[ (\rho - \sigma) \frac{1}{L_\rho + s R_\sigma}(\rho-\sigma) \bigr] d\mu(s),
\end{align*}
where $\mu$ is a positive measure on $(0,\infty)$ such that $\int_0^\infty \frac{1}{1+s} d\mu(s) < \infty$. We study the derivatives of the $f$-divergence. Let $\rho_t = \sigma + t(\rho - \sigma)$.

\paragraph*{Main problem}

Calculate $\frac{d}{dt} D^{\mathrm{std}}_f(\rho_t \|\sigma)$ at $t = 0.5$. Please be as accurate as possible.
