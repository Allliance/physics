Question: Solution review notes
Answer:
The original solution was largely consistent but relied on the assumption that the magnetic order comprised only the two reported Fourier components, based on which it concluded that no solution existed. A spatially uniform component (zero frequency) is also a valid Fourier component of the magnetization. This component in neutron scattering appears at the same positions as the nuclear Bragg peaks, rather than manifesting as an additional peak, and thus may be present without having been reported. By including this term, the updated solution identifies the BNS 20.33 magnetic space group, which I now present as the answer, along with the subgroups that still allow for a net moment along the c-axis.
