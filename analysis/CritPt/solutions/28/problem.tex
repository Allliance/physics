\subsubsection*{Problem}
\paragraph*{Problem setup}
Consider a four-dimensional hypercubic lattice with lattice spacing $a$ and nearest-neighbor hopping amplitude $t$. The system includes an on-site Hubbard interaction of strength $U$, and the chemical potential is near the bottom of the conduction band.

The Hubbard interaction gives a correction to the real part of the finite-frequency paramagnetic conductivity.

\paragraph*{Main problem}

{\color{blue}
At zero temperature and to second order in $U$, define the low-frequency quasiparticle and transport rates on the Fermi surface by
\[
\Gamma_{\mathrm{qp}}(k_F,\omega)
 =A_{\mathrm{qp}}(k_F)\,\omega^2+o(\omega^2),
\qquad
\Gamma_{\mathrm{tr}}(k_F,\omega)
 =A_{\mathrm{tr}}(k_F)\,\omega^2+o(\omega^2)
\]
as $\omega\to0^+$. Also let $\delta\operatorname{Re}\sigma^{\mathrm{reg}}_{yy}(0^+)$ denote the interaction-induced regular (non-Drude) conductivity per unit physical product of the two two-dimensional momentum-space measures.

Find the leading dilute-limit exponents $\gamma_\sigma$, $\gamma_{\mathrm{qp}}$, and $\gamma_{\mathrm{tr}}$ defined by
\[
\delta\operatorname{Re}\sigma^{\mathrm{reg}}_{yy}(0^+)\propto k_F^{\gamma_\sigma},
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{qp}}(k_F)\propto k_F^{\gamma_{\mathrm{qp}}},
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{tr}}(k_F)\propto k_F^{\gamma_{\mathrm{tr}}}.
\]
}
