\subsubsection*{Solution}
\paragraph*{Identify the low-rank ensemble}

Define the Hermitian random bilinears

\[
Q_a=i\sum_{i,j=1}^{N}C_{ij}^{a}\chi_i\chi_j.
\]

Then the Hamiltonian can be written as

\[
H=\frac12\sum_{a=1}^{N}Q_a^2.
\]

In the conventional low-rank SYK notation

\[
H=-\frac12\sum_{a=1}^{R}\lambda_a Q_a^2,
\]

this means that $R=N$ and every nonzero coupling-matrix eigenvalue is
$\lambda_a=-1$. Thus the rescaled rank is $\gamma=R/N=1$, and the model is
the Class-IV low-rank SYK fixed point, rather than the ordinary SYK$_4$
fixed point. This sign and rank identification is essential
(\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.10173}{Kim, Cao, and Altman}).

\paragraph*{Infrared scaling dimension}

For Class IV, the fermion scaling dimension $\Delta\in(0,1/4)$ obeys

\[
\gamma=
\frac{(2\Delta-1)\bigl(\sec(2\pi\Delta)-1\bigr)}{8\Delta-2}.
\]

At $\gamma=1$, this equation is solved exactly by

\[
\Delta=\frac16,
\]

because $\sec(2\pi\Delta)=\sec(\pi/3)=2$. The same $M/N=1$ saddle was
identified with the cubic $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetric SYK saddle by
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.07081}{Bi et al.}; in the supersymmetric
notation the odd supercharge order is $\widehat q=3$ and
$\Delta=1/(2\widehat q)$.

\paragraph*{Residual entropy}

The large-$N$ residual entropy of this saddle is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.08917}{Fu, Gaiotto, Maldacena, and Sachdev}

\[
\frac{S_0}{N}
=\frac12\ln\!\left[2\cos(\pi\Delta)\right]
=\frac12\ln\!\left[2\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]
=\frac14\ln 3.
\]

Numerically,

\[
\frac{S_0}{N}=0.274653072167\ldots,
\]

which rounds to $0.2747$ at four decimal places. The scale $J$ only sets the
energy and temperature units and does not change this dimensionless entropy.
