\section{Statement}

A three-level atom with ground states $\ket b,\ket d$ and excited state $\ket e$
sits in a lossless cavity resonant with the bright transition
$\ket b\!\leftrightarrow\!\ket e$.  With $\hbar=1$,
\begin{equation}
  \hat H=\frac{g}{2}\left(\ketbra{b}{e}\hat a^{\dagger}
                         +\ketbra{e}{b}\hat a\right),
  \qquad
  \hat J=\sqrt{\gamma}\,\ketbra{d}{e},
  \qquad
  \dot{\hat\rho}=-\ii[\hat H,\hat\rho]
  +\hat J\hat\rho\hat J^{\dagger}
  -\tfrac12\{\hat J^{\dagger}\hat J,\hat\rho\},
  \label{eq:me}
\end{equation}
and $\hat\rho_0=\ketbra{b}{b}\otimes\ketbra{\alpha}{\alpha}$.  We want
$\bra{n'}\hat\rho_{c,\rm ss}\ket{n}$.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=red!5,colframe=red!55!black,
  title=\textbf{Careful: $\mathcal L\hat\rho=0$ does not determine the answer}]
The Liouvillian of Eq.~\eqref{eq:me} has a massively degenerate kernel: every
$\ketbra{d}{d}\otimes\hat\sigma$ with arbitrary cavity state $\hat\sigma$ is
stationary, and so is $\ketbra{b,0}{b,0}$.  Setting the left-hand side to zero
therefore gives a family of solutions, and the physical steady state is
selected by the \emph{initial condition}.  The clean way to impose ``LHS
$=0$'' is to integrate Eq.~\eqref{eq:me} over all time,
\begin{equation}
  \int_0^{\infty}\!\dd t\,\dot{\hat\rho}=\hat\rho(\infty)-\hat\rho(0),
  \label{eq:integrate}
\end{equation}
which turns the equations of motion into an \emph{algebraic} linear system for
the time integrals $\hat M_{xy}=\int_0^{\infty}\hat\rho_{xy}(t)\,\dd t$.  That
is the derivation below.  (In the same vein, \texttt{qutip.steadystate()}
returns an arbitrary kernel element here and must not be used --- the numerics
in Sec.~\ref{sec:qutip} propagate to long times instead.)
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{Derivation}

\subsection{Block equations of motion}

Write $\hat\rho=\sum_{x,y\in\{b,e,d\}}\ketbra{x}{y}\otimes\hat\rho_{xy}$, where
each $\hat\rho_{xy}$ is an operator on the cavity.  Substituting into
Eq.~\eqref{eq:me} and collecting blocks,
\begin{align}
  \dot{\hat\rho}_{bb}&=-\tfrac{\ii g}{2}
    \left(\hat a^{\dagger}\hat\rho_{eb}-\hat\rho_{be}\hat a\right),
  \label{eq:bb}\\
  \dot{\hat\rho}_{be}&=-\tfrac{\ii g}{2}
    \left(\hat a^{\dagger}\hat\rho_{ee}-\hat\rho_{bb}\hat a^{\dagger}\right)
    -\tfrac{\gamma}{2}\hat\rho_{be},
  \label{eq:be}\\
  \dot{\hat\rho}_{eb}&=-\tfrac{\ii g}{2}
    \left(\hat a\hat\rho_{bb}-\hat\rho_{ee}\hat a\right)
    -\tfrac{\gamma}{2}\hat\rho_{eb},
  \label{eq:eb}\\
  \dot{\hat\rho}_{ee}&=-\tfrac{\ii g}{2}
    \left(\hat a\hat\rho_{be}-\hat\rho_{eb}\hat a^{\dagger}\right)
    -\gamma\hat\rho_{ee},
  \label{eq:ee}\\
  \dot{\hat\rho}_{dd}&=\gamma\hat\rho_{ee}.
  \label{eq:dd}
\end{align}
The blocks $\hat\rho_{bd},\hat\rho_{ed}$ and their adjoints start at zero and
are never sourced ($\hat J$ produces only $\ketbra{d}{d}$ terms, and $\hat H$
does not touch $\ket d$), so they stay zero: \textbf{no atomic coherence with
the dark state is ever generated}, and the cavity state factorises as
$\rhoc=\hat\rho_{bb}+\hat\rho_{ee}+\hat\rho_{dd}$.

\subsection{Integrating over all time}

As $t\to\infty$ every excitation ends in $\ket d$ except the piece that started
in the dark corner $\ket{b,0}$ (for which $\hat a\ket{b,0}=0$, so it does not
evolve).  Hence
\begin{equation}
  \hat\rho_{bb}(\infty)=\rho^{0}_{00}\ketbra{0}{0},\qquad
  \hat\rho_{be}(\infty)=\hat\rho_{ee}(\infty)=0,\qquad
  \rhoc^{\rm ss}=\rho^{0}_{00}\ketbra{0}{0}+\hat\rho_{dd}(\infty),
  \label{eq:asymptotics}
\end{equation}
with $\rho^{0}_{n'n}=\bra{n'}\rhoc(0)\ket{n}$.  Integrating
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:bb}--\eqref{eq:ee} using \eqref{eq:integrate} and
\eqref{eq:asymptotics} gives four algebraic equations for
$\hat M_{bb},\hat M_{be},\hat M_{eb},\hat M_{ee}$, and \eqref{eq:dd} gives the
answer directly:
\begin{equation}
  \hat\rho_{dd}(\infty)=\gamma\hat M_{ee}.
  \label{eq:answer0}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Solving, manifold by manifold}

The operators $\hat a,\hat a^{\dagger}$ shift Fock indices by one, so the system
closes on each pair $(n,n')$ with $n,n'\ge1$.  Writing
\begin{equation}
  \hat M_{bb}\!\supset\!u\ketbra{n}{n'},\quad
  \hat M_{ee}\!\supset\!v\ketbra{n-1}{n'-1},\quad
  \hat M_{be}\!\supset\!w\ketbra{n}{n'-1},\quad
  \hat M_{eb}\!\supset\!x\ketbra{n-1}{n'},
\end{equation}
and abbreviating $\Omega_n=\tfrac{g}{2}\sqrt n$ (the matrix element
$\bra{e,n-1}\hat H\ket{b,n}$), Eqs.~\eqref{eq:ee}, \eqref{eq:be},
\eqref{eq:eb}, \eqref{eq:bb} become
\begin{align}
  \gamma v&=-\ii\left(\Omega_n w-\Omega_{n'}x\right), &
  \tfrac{\gamma}{2}w&=-\ii\left(\Omega_n v-\Omega_{n'}u\right),
  \label{eq:sys1}\\
  \tfrac{\gamma}{2}x&=-\ii\left(\Omega_n u-\Omega_{n'}v\right), &
  -\rho^{0}_{nn'}&=-\ii\left(\Omega_n x-\Omega_{n'}w\right).
  \label{eq:sys2}
\end{align}
Eliminating $w,x$ from the two right-hand equations and inserting into the two
left-hand ones gives, with $S=\Omega_n^{2}+\Omega_{n'}^{2}$ and
$P=\Omega_n\Omega_{n'}$,
\begin{equation}
  v\left(\gamma^{2}+2S\right)=4Pu,
  \qquad
  \rho^{0}_{nn'}=\frac{2}{\gamma}\left(Su-2Pv\right)
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \gamma v=\rho^{0}_{nn'}\,\frac{2\gamma^{2}P}
  {2\left(S^{2}-4P^{2}\right)+\gamma^{2}S}.
  \label{eq:solve}
\end{equation}
With $\Omega_n^{2}=g^{2}n/4$ one has $S=\tfrac{g^{2}}{4}(n+n')$,
$P=\tfrac{g^{2}}{4}\sqrt{nn'}$ and $S^{2}-4P^{2}=\tfrac{g^{4}}{16}(n-n')^{2}$,
so all powers of $g$ cancel except one ratio:
\begin{equation}
  \gamma v=\rho^{0}_{nn'}\,K_{nn'},
  \qquad
  K_{nn'}=\frac{2\sqrt{nn'}}
  {\dfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}}\left(n-n'\right)^{2}+n+n'} .
  \label{eq:K}
\end{equation}
Note $K_{nn}=1$: every $\ket{b,n\ge1}$ ends up in $\ket{d,n-1}$ with
probability one, as it must.

\subsection{Result}

Equation~\eqref{eq:K} says
$\bra{n-1}\hat\rho_{dd}(\infty)\ket{n'-1}=\rho^{0}_{nn'}K_{nn'}$.  Relabelling
$n\to n'+1$, $n'\to n+1$ and restoring the dark corner of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:asymptotics}:

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=blue!4,colframe=blue!45!black,title=Result --- any
input state]
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;
  \bra{n'}\rhoc^{\rm ss}\ket{n}
  =\rho^{0}_{00}\,\delta_{n0}\delta_{n'0}
  +\rho^{0}_{n'+1,n+1}\;
  \frac{2\sqrt{(n+1)(n'+1)}}
  {\dfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}}\left(n-n'\right)^{2}+n+n'+2}\;}
  \label{eq:main}
\end{equation}
equivalently, in operator form with $\Had$ the elementwise (Hadamard) product
in the Fock basis,
\begin{equation}
  \rhoc^{\rm ss}=\rho^{0}_{00}\ketbra{0}{0}
  +F\Had\left(\hat a\,\rhoc(0)\,\hat a^{\dagger}\right),
  \qquad
  F_{n'n}=\frac{2}{\dfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}}(n-n')^{2}+n+n'+2}.
  \label{eq:hadamard}
\end{equation}
\end{tcolorbox}

\noindent
For the coherent state of the problem, $\rho^{0}_{n'n}=\ee^{-|\alpha|^{2}}
\alpha^{n'}(\alpha^{*})^{n}/\sqrt{n'!\,n!}$, and Eq.~\eqref{eq:main} becomes
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;
  \bra{n'}\rhoc^{\rm ss}\ket{n}
  =\ee^{-|\alpha|^{2}}\left[\delta_{n0}\delta_{n'0}
  +\frac{2\,|\alpha|^{2}\,\alpha^{n'}(\alpha^{*})^{n}}
  {\sqrt{n!\,n'!}\;\left[\dfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}}(n-n')^{2}+n+n'+2\right]}
  \right]\;}
  \label{eq:coherent}
\end{equation}
where $\ee^{-|\alpha|^2}\equiv\exp(-|\alpha|^2)$.

\section{Physical content and checks}

\paragraph{Trace.}
$\sum_n\bra{n}\rhoc^{\rm ss}\ket{n}
=\ee^{-|\alpha|^{2}}\bigl[1+\sum_n|\alpha|^{2(n+1)}/(n+1)!\bigr]
=\ee^{-|\alpha|^{2}}\ee^{|\alpha|^{2}}=1$.

\paragraph{The diagonal is trivial and $g,\gamma$-independent.}
Setting $n=n'$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:main} gives
$\bra{n}\rhoc^{\rm ss}\ket{n}=\rho^{0}_{n+1,n+1}+\rho^{0}_{00}\delta_{n0}$:
exactly one photon is removed unless the cavity was already empty.  All the
physics of $g$ and $\gamma$ lives in the \emph{coherences}.

\paragraph{The coherence-reduction factor.}
Dividing by $\sqrt{\rho^{\rm ss}_{nn}\rho^{\rm ss}_{n'n'}}$, the normalised
coherence of the initially pure state is multiplied by
\begin{equation}
  D_{n'n}=\frac{2\sqrt{(n+1)(n'+1)}}
  {\dfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}}(n-n')^{2}+n+n'+2}\;\le\;1,
  \qquad D_{nn}=1,
  \label{eq:D}
\end{equation}
by AM--GM.  Two limits:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item $g/\gamma\to\infty$: $D\to0$ for $n\neq n'$ --- complete decoherence in
the Fock basis.  The emitted photon is a clock: the vacuum Rabi frequency
$\Omega_n=\tfrac{g}{2}\sqrt n$ is resolved within the excited-state lifetime, so
the emission time carries full which-path information about $n$.
\item $g/\gamma\to0$: $D\to2\sqrt{(n+1)(n'+1)}/(n+n'+2)<1$ --- decoherence
survives even in the Purcell limit, now because the \emph{rates}
$\Gamma_n=4\Omega_n^{2}/\gamma=g^{2}n/\gamma$ differ with $n$: an exponential
decay with an $n$-dependent time constant still leaks which-path information.
\end{itemize}
For example $D_{01}=2\sqrt2/(3+\tfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}})$: $0.9412$ at
$g/\gamma=0.1$, $0.5657$ at $g/\gamma=2$, $0.0534$ at $g/\gamma=10$.

\section{QuTiP cross-check}
\label{sec:qutip}

\texttt{answer.py} propagates Eq.~\eqref{eq:me} with \texttt{qutip.mesolve} to
$T=60/\min(\gamma/4,\,g^{2}/\gamma)$ (the slowest relaxation rate of the $n=1$
manifold), traces out the atom, and compares with Eq.~\eqref{eq:main}.  The
same checks are also available as the standalone script
\texttt{qutip\_check.py}, in which the analytic formula is re-implemented from
scratch --- so the agreement below is not an artefact of shared code --- and
which additionally shows the degeneracy of the Liouvillian kernel explicitly
($N^{2}+1+2N=49$ zero eigenvalues at $N=6$, with
\texttt{qutip.steadystate()} failing outright on the singular matrix),
the exponential convergence in $T$, and a printed $5\times5$ corner of both
matrices.

Two remarks make the comparison exact rather than approximate.  First, a Fock
cutoff at $N$ is \emph{not} an approximation here: $\hat H$ only converts
$\ket{b,n}$ into $\ket{e,n-1}$, so the photon number never increases and
$\mathrm{span}\{\ket n:n<N\}$ is invariant.  Second, the same truncated and
renormalised amplitude vector is fed to both sides, so the column
``formula \eqref{eq:main}'' below is a pure test of the physics, limited only by
the integrator tolerances ($\texttt{atol}=10^{-12}$,
$\texttt{rtol}=10^{-10}$); the ``closed form'' column additionally carries the
$N\to\infty$ truncation error of Eq.~\eqref{eq:coherent}, listed separately.

\section{Bonus: squeezed input}

Nothing in Sec.~2 used the coherent-state amplitudes --- Eq.~\eqref{eq:main} is
linear in $\rhoc(0)$ and holds for \emph{any} input, pure or mixed.  For a
squeezed vacuum $\ket\zeta=\hat S(r\ee^{\ii\theta})\ket0$,
\begin{equation}
  s_{2m}\equiv\langle 2m|\zeta\rangle
  =\frac{\left(-\ee^{\ii\theta}\tanh r\right)^{m}\sqrt{(2m)!}}
        {2^{m}\,m!\,\sqrt{\cosh r}},
  \qquad s_{2m+1}=0,
\end{equation}
so that
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;
  \bra{n'}\rhoc^{\rm ss}\ket{n}
  =|s_0|^{2}\delta_{n0}\delta_{n'0}
  +s_{n'+1}s^{*}_{n+1}\,
  \frac{2\sqrt{(n+1)(n'+1)}}
  {\dfrac{g^{2}}{2\gamma^{2}}(n-n')^{2}+n+n'+2}\;}
  \label{eq:squeezed}
\end{equation}
with the striking consequence that \textbf{the steady state is supported on
\emph{odd} photon numbers plus the vacuum}: the squeezed vacuum contains only
even $n$, and exactly one photon is always removed.  The surviving vacuum
weight is $|s_0|^{2}=1/\cosh r$.  Table~\ref{tab:squeezed} confirms
Eq.~\eqref{eq:squeezed}, and the last two rows of Table~\ref{tab:sqcoh} confirm
the general formula \eqref{eq:main} for a squeezed \emph{coherent} input
$\hat D(\alpha)\hat S(r)\ket0$, for which both parities are populated.
